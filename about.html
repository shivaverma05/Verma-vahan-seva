Our Story

Verma Digital Studio was founded in 2021 by Rahul Verma, a full-stack developer with a passion for making quality digital tools accessible to everyone in India.

What started as a small side project selling HTML templates has grown into a thriving marketplace with 500+ products, 2800+ customers, and a team of talented developers and designers.

Our mission is simple: provide Indian developers, entrepreneurs, and businesses with premium digital products at affordable prices, backed by world-class support.

Today, Verma Digital Studio is trusted by freelancers, startups, and enterprises across India and beyond to power their digital projects.
